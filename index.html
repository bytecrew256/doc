How the Tracker Works MamaCycle turns two simple dates into a full picture of your cycle. No guesswork, no complicated math. Step 1: You Log Your Last Period When you sign up or open the tracker, you enter the date your last period started. This is your starting point, and it's the first real signal your body gives us. Step 2: You Log Your Current Period When your next period begins, you enter that date. MamaCycle now has two real data points from your own body, not averages or estimates pulled from someone else. Step 3: MamaCycle Calculates Your Cycle Using the gap between those two dates, MamaCycle works out: Your cycle length: how many days your body actually takes from the start of one period to the start of the next. Your next expected period: predicted from your real rhythm. Your fertile window: the days you're most likely to conceive. Your safe days: the lower-risk days in your cycle. The more cycles you log, the sharper your predictions become. MamaCycle learns your pattern, not someone else's. Step 4: Your Calendar Does the Rest Everything is laid out on a marked calendar so you can see your cycle at a glance: Period days, clearly marked so you're never caught off guard. Fertile window, highlighted so you know when your chances of conception are highest. Safe days, marked out so you can plan with confidence. No spreadsheets. No counting on your fingers. Just open the calendar and see exactly where you are and what's coming next. Why This Approach Works Many cycle apps rely on averages, assuming every user has a 28-day cycle. But real bodies don't work that way. Your cycle might be 26 days, 32 days, or shift slightly month to month. By using your own logged dates, MamaCycle builds predictions around your actual rhythm. That means fewer surprises, more accurate forecasts, and a calendar that genuinely reflects you. Understanding Your Predictions Once you've logged at least two cycles, MamaCycle begins generating predictions. Here's what each one means and how to use it. Cycle Length Your cycle length is the number of days between the first day of one period and the first day of the next. A typical cycle falls between 21 and 35 days, but everyone is different. MamaCycle tracks yours over time and adjusts predictions as your pattern becomes clearer. Next Expected Period Based on your average cycle length, MamaCycle estimates when your next period will begin. This date updates automatically as you log new cycles, so it stays accurate even if your rhythm shifts. Fertile Window Your fertile window is the span of days when conception is most likely. This typically includes the days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. MamaCycle highlights this window on your calendar so you can plan accordingly, whether you're trying to conceive or trying to avoid it. Safe Days Safe days are the days outside your fertile window when the chance of conception is lower. These are marked clearly on your calendar. It's important to remember that no method based on cycle tracking is 100 percent effective on its own. Safe days are a guide, not a guarantee. How Predictions Improve Over Time Every cycle you log gives MamaCycle more data. After three or four cycles, predictions become noticeably more accurate. After six months, your calendar reflects a strong, personalized pattern. The key is consistency: log your period start date each month, and MamaCycle handles the rest. Symptom Logging Tracking your cycle isn't just about dates. How you feel matters too, and MamaCycle gives you a simple way to record it. Each day, you can log: Flow: light, medium, heavy, or spotting. Cramps: none, mild, moderate, or severe. Mood: happy, irritable, anxious, calm, and more. Energy levels: high, normal, or low. Other symptoms: headaches, bloating, breast tenderness, acne, and more. Over time, these logs reveal patterns. You might notice that your energy dips two days before your period, or that cramps are worse in certain months. This kind of insight helps you plan your life around your body, rather than the other way around. Your symptom history is stored securely and remains available whenever you need to review it.
