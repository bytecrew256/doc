Getting Started

Sign Up

  1. Go to mamacycle.com.
  2. Click Sign Up and enter your email, password, and due date (if applicable).
  3. Verify your email address via the confirmation link.

Once your account is verified, you'll be guided through a short setup where you can enter your last period date to begin tracking right away.

Log In

  • Enter your email and password on the login page.
  • Enable Remember Me for persistent sessions on your personal device.

Prerequisites

  • A valid email address.
  • A supported browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge, latest version recommended).
  • Optional: a mobile number if you'd like SMS reminders.

Setting Up Your Profile

After signing up, you can personalize your experience by adding:

  • Your typical cycle length, if you already know it.
  • Your average period duration.
  • Reminder preferences for periods, fertile windows, and medications.

The more information you provide upfront, the faster MamaCycle can tailor predictions to your body.

How the Tracker Works

MamaCycle turns two simple dates into a full picture of your cycle. No guesswork, no complicated math.

Step 1: You Log Your Last Period

When you sign up or open the tracker, you enter the date your last period started. This is your starting point, and it's the first real signal your body gives us.

Step 2: You Log Your Current Period

When your next period begins, you enter that date. MamaCycle now has two real data points from your own body, not averages or estimates pulled from someone else.

Step 3: MamaCycle Calculates Your Cycle

Using the gap between those two dates, MamaCycle works out:

  • Your cycle length: how many days your body actually takes from the start of one period to the start of the next.
  • Your next expected period: predicted from your real rhythm.
  • Your fertile window: the days you're most likely to conceive.
  • Your safe days: the lower-risk days in your cycle.

The more cycles you log, the sharper your predictions become. MamaCycle learns your pattern, not someone else's.

Step 4: Your Calendar Does the Rest

Everything is laid out on a marked calendar so you can see your cycle at a glance.

Why This Approach Works

Many cycle apps rely on averages, assuming every user has a 28-day cycle. But real bodies don't work that way. Your cycle might be 26 days, 32 days, or shift slightly month to month. By using your own logged dates, MamaCycle builds predictions around your actual rhythm. That means fewer surprises, more accurate forecasts, and a calendar that genuinely reflects you.

Understanding Your Predictions

Once you've logged at least two cycles, MamaCycle begins generating predictions. Here's what each one means and how to use it.

Cycle Length

Your cycle length is the number of days between the first day of one period and the first day of the next. A typical cycle falls between 21 and 35 days, but everyone is different. MamaCycle tracks yours over time and adjusts predictions as your pattern becomes clearer.

Next Expected Period

Based on your average cycle length, MamaCycle estimates when your next period will begin. This date updates automatically as you log new cycles, so it stays accurate even if your rhythm shifts.

Fertile Window

Your fertile window is the span of days when conception is most likely. This typically includes the days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. MamaCycle highlights this window on your calendar so you can plan accordingly, whether you're trying to conceive or trying to avoid it.

Safe Days

Safe days are the days outside your fertile window when the chance of conception is lower. These are marked clearly on your calendar. It's important to remember that no method based on cycle tracking is 100 percent effective on its own. Safe days are a guide, not a guarantee.

How Predictions Improve Over Time

Every cycle you log gives MamaCycle more data. After three or four cycles, predictions become noticeably more accurate. After six months, your calendar reflects a strong, personalized pattern. The key is consistency: log your period start date each month, and MamaCycle handles the rest.

Symptom Logging

Tracking your cycle isn't just about dates. How you feel matters too, and MamaCycle gives you a simple way to record it.

Each day, you can log:

  • Flow: light, medium, heavy, or spotting.
  • Cramps: none, mild, moderate, or severe.
  • Mood: happy, irritable, anxious, calm, and more.
  • Energy levels: high, normal, or low.
  • Other symptoms: headaches, bloating, breast tenderness, acne, and more.

Over time, these logs reveal patterns. You might notice that your energy dips two days before your period, or that cramps are worse in certain months. This kind of insight helps you plan your life around your body, rather than the other way around.

Your symptom history is stored securely and remains available whenever you need to review it.

Account Management

FAQ

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell personal information. See the Privacy and Security section for more.

Can I use MamaCycle on multiple devices?

Yes. Your data syncs automatically across all devices.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Your data remains in the cloud. Reinstall the app and log in to restore it.

How accurate are the predictions?

Accuracy improves with every cycle you log. Because MamaCycle uses your own real dates instead of generic averages, predictions reflect your actual rhythm and get sharper over time.

What if my cycle is irregular?

MamaCycle is designed to adapt. If your cycle length varies, the app tracks those variations and adjusts predictions accordingly. The more data you log, the better it understands your pattern, even if that pattern isn't perfectly consistent.

Can I use MamaCycle to avoid pregnancy?

MamaCycle can help you identify your fertile window and safe days, but no cycle-based method should be relied on as your only form of contraception. Speak with a healthcare provider about the best approach for your needs.

Can I use MamaCycle if I'm trying to conceive?

Absolutely. The fertile window feature is designed to help you identify your most likely days for conception. Many users rely on MamaCycle specifically for this purpose.

Is MamaCycle suitable for teenagers?

Yes. MamaCycle is designed for anyone who menstruates, regardless of age. The Health Library includes content suitable for younger users who are just learning about their cycles.

Do I need to log every day?

No. The most important log is your period start date. Symptom logging is optional but helps build a fuller picture over time.

What happens if I forget to log a period?

You can add past dates at any time. MamaCycle will update your predictions once the new data is entered.
