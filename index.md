## Getting Started

### Sign Up
1. Go to [mamacycle.com](https://mamacycle.com).
2. Click **Sign Up** and enter your email, password, and due date (if applicable).
3. Verify your email address via the confirmation link.

### Log In
- Use your email and password on the login page.
- Enable **Remember Me** for persistent sessions.

## Account Management

### Change Password
- Go to **Settings → Security**.
- Enter your current password, then new password.
- Click **Save**.

### Delete Account
- Go to **Settings → Privacy**.
- Click **Delete Account** (this is permanent).

## Troubleshooting

**Issue:** Can't receive SMS notifications.  
**Fix:** Check your mobile number in **Settings → Notifications** and ensure carrier allows shortcodes.

**Issue:** App is slow.  
**Fix:** Clear cache or update to the latest version.

## FAQ

**Is my data private?**  
Yes, all data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell personal information.

**Can I use Mamacycle on multiple devices?**  
Yes, your data syncs automatically.

**What happens if I uninstall the app?**  
Your data remains in the cloud. Reinstall and log in to restore.
